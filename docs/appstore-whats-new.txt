Winnow keeps your bitcoin on your phone and reads the chain straight from ordinary Bitcoin nodes. No account, no server, no company in the middle.

This first release:
• Send and receive with fresh Taproot addresses.
• Saved recipients: name a past payment and choose the recipient from Send. Save a payment card to use a fresh address each time.
• Shared savings: hold money together with the people you choose, and decide how many of you must approve before it moves.
• Advanced mode for the test network, your own peers, chain verification and the raw multisig tools.
