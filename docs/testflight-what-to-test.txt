Saved recipients and shared savings

Send a payment, then open it in Wallet and save the recipient. Rename them and restart the app: the payment should show the new name. Remove them from Saved recipients in Send: the old payment should keep its name. You can save them again from that payment.

Open Receive and tap Share my card. Give it a name and share it with another device. There, open Send → Saved recipients → Add and paste the card. It should say “Fresh address each payment.” Pay twice and check that each review names the recipient and uses a different address. A saved fixed address should explain that repeated payments can be linked.

Create shared savings from Wallet and choose the co-owners. Share the account card and add it on the other phone before funding it. Send a small amount, wait for confirmation, then use Ask for approval and Approve a request. Finish sends the payment once enough people have approved.

Beginner mode

The wallet has three tabs: Wallet, Send, Settings. Turn on Advanced mode in Settings to see network and fee controls. Custom account creation appears in Wallet alongside your other accounts. Turn Advanced off again: those controls disappear and your saved settings remain.
